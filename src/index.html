When each site has power and a link to Earth

Sun height above the terrain horizon

Above 0° the centre of the Sun clears the local terrain. At the pole the Sun skims the horizon, so a degree of terrain decides between power and darkness.

Earth height above the terrain horizon

Earth hangs almost still in the lunar sky and wobbles a few degrees each month (libration). Below 0° no direct-to-Earth link is possible.
